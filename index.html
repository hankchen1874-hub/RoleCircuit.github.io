02 / BROWSER EXTENSION

The form fills itself. You keep the pen.

The RoleCircuit Chrome extension fills application forms on the major hiring platforms — Greenhouse, Lever, Workday and more — using your own answer bank. It types your details, picks sensible defaults for the awkward questions, and then stops. It never clicks submit.

  • ✓Your details, filled from your answer bank
  • ✓EEO questions answered "Decline to self-identify" by default
  • ✓If a form changes, it adapts and keeps going
  • ☝Submit is always, only, your click

03 / SIGNAL RADAR

Know who's hiring before the job is posted.

By the time a posting goes live, hundreds apply. RoleCircuit watches the signals that come first — funding announcements, regulatory filings, founder chatter — and alerts you days before the role exists. You show up before the crowd.

  • WATCHES Funding rounds & company filings
  • WATCHES Founder & team hiring chatter
  • ALERTS Straight to your Telegram, days early
04 / EVIDENCE, NOT VIBES

Apply with receipts. Skip with reasons.

No black-box scores. For every role, RoleCircuit shows what the posting asks for next to the line of your own experience that answers it — and it's just as honest about what doesn't fit, so you can skip without second-guessing.

06 / WHY LOCAL MATTERS

Your career data never leaves your machine.

01

Runs where you live

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Your resume, your applications, your replies — stored on your machine, not on someone's server.

02

Open to inspection

Fully open source under the MIT license. Read every line, audit what it does with your data, change what you don't like.

03

Never acts without you

It fills forms but never submits them. It drafts messages but never sends them. Every irreversible action is yours alone.
